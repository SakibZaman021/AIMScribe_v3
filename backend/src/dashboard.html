Your details

Your official name, the ID AIMS LAB gave you, and your phone. The researchers who look after your hospitals see them with every alert, so they can call you; the attendance sheet for salaries uses them too.

My shift

What the circles mean: 🔴 act now - someone must go and fix it (press Seen) · 🟠 unusual, nothing to do now - kept for the log · 🟢 back to normal · ⚪ over, not fixed (the PC was switched off first) · 📋 live board · 📊 shift summary

While you are on shift, Telegram alerts you at once about anything unusual at your place.

Alerts on your phone

Telegram, free. Do this once, on your phone.

Install Telegram from the Play Store, press Connect Telegram, then press Start in Telegram. Keep Telegram's notifications on. For an urgent alert, press Seen in Telegram when you are dealing with it.

My alerts

Urgent alerts not marked seen in 15 minutes (here, or with Seen in Telegram) also go to the other collectors of your place and to AIMS LAB.

The hospitals you look after

Loading

You see and download every hospital as before. For these you also get every alert the data collectors get, with the collector on shift and their phone and Telegram; word when they press Seen; a reminder when a problem is still not fixed after 30, 60 and 90 minutes; word when it is fixed; and a summary of each shift at 14:30 and 20:30, with an Excel workbook for each of your hospitals. Nothing is sent at night: what happens then is in the next summary.

PCs that could not join

          The last 30 days: the code each PC was given by CMED, and why it
            was refused. Link the code to a clinic and the PC joins at its next patient.
